burin engraving machine

Landscape engravings, cut line by line from a single number.

  • every seed is a new plate
  • drawn by pencil, then pen, in about a minute
  • after Gustave Doré
$ burin engrave --seed … # choosing a scene
[....................] 0% waiting

how it works

  1. 01scene

    The seed picks a lake and castle, a dark wood, a gorge, a ruined abbey or a stormy headland, sometimes by moonlight, sometimes as a tall plate.

  2. 02pencil

    Two pencils search out every contour with light, overshooting strokes.

  3. 03tone map

    A hidden map sets how dark each point should be: clouds lit at the rim, faceted rock, light pooling on the focus.

  4. 04hatching

    Evenly spaced streamlines follow each form. Four pens build tone in three passes, light to dark.

  5. 05details

    Cracks, masonry, ivy, leaves, grass, riders and pilgrims.

  6. 06print

    Ink spreads into dense shading and speckles in the blacks, like a pulled proof.
